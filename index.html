Questions

Frequently asked

How long does a typical project take?

Most projects range from 6-12 weeks depending on scope. Brand identity projects typically take 4-6 weeks, while full website builds can take 8-12 weeks. We'll provide a detailed timeline during discovery.

Do you work with international clients?

Absolutely. We've worked with clients across North America, Europe, Asia, and Australia. Our process is designed to work seamlessly across time zones with async communication and regular video check-ins.

What's included in your support packages?

Support includes bug fixes, minor content updates, performance monitoring, and technical assistance. Professional tier includes 3 months, Enterprise includes 6 months with priority response times.

Can you work within our existing brand guidelines?

Yes. We can work within established brand systems or help evolve them as needed. We're experienced in both creating new identities and extending existing ones.

What if we need revisions after launch?

All projects include a defined number of revision rounds. Post-launch changes can be handled through our support packages or on an hourly basis depending on scope.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. We typically structure payments in milestones: 30% upfront, 40% at midpoint, and 30% at completion. Custom arrangements can be discussed for enterprise projects.
